Biography

Crimson Bridge are a metal band from Almada, with sonic influences that spread across the entire genre’s spectrum, without letting themselves be limited by its boundaries. The group started in 2016 with Renato Guerreiro and Daniel Almeida on guitar and Nuno Cruz on bass. At this point, the band was searching for a drummer and eventually found Bernardo Rebelo. By the end of that year, Filipe Alaiz also joined the group as a vocalist and the first lineup was finally completed. In July 2017, the band performed for the first time live in concert at the historic venue “Ponto de Encontro”, in Cacilhas. In October of the same year, the band played at the “Mostra de Música Moderna de Almada” (Almada’s Modern Music Showcase). This concert led to an invitation for the band to represent its home town at the “Festival da Liberdade”, which would take place the following year.
